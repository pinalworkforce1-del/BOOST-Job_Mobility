Start Somewhere. Learn What You Bring.

This experience is not trying to predict your career. It helps you notice the strengths you already use, test them in realistic work situations, and decide which skills you want your next job to help you develop.

Honeycomb idea: Experience reveals strengths. Strengths build skills. Skills create opportunity. Your next job does not have to be your forever career—it should help you earn, learn, and gather better evidence about yourself.

Protect Your Pathway

Some behaviors are not career preferences. They are the foundation that helps you keep opportunities open wherever you work.

You do not have to love every foundation behavior. You do need to use it when the work requires it.
